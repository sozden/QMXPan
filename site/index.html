QMXPan

Panadapter for the QRP Labs QMX / QMX+ on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3

Full

For stations with WiFi.

  • Spectrum, waterfall, CW and FT8 decoders, meters
  • Internet clock, DX cluster spots, propagation page
  • Web settings and firmware updates over WiFi
Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Lite

For portable use without WiFi.

  • Spectrum, waterfall, CW and FT8 decoders, meters
  • Clock set by hand on the screen, kept accurate by FT8
  • No WiFi: more free memory, simpler start-up
Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
Before you click: connect the board's UART USB port to this computer. On a first install tick Erase device. When updating leave it unticked to keep your WiFi, callsign and settings.
After installing, connect the board's USB OTG port to the QMX and switch the QMX's 12 V off and on once.
